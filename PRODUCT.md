# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Internal teams tracking their own and client websites. Public visitors evaluate the open source product and its self-host deployment.

## Product Purpose

Understand traffic and goal completions across organisations containing multiple projects, using an installation the operator controls.

## Positioning

TypeScript analytics running in the operator’s Cloudflare account with Workers, D1 and a cookieless tracker.

## Operating Context

Public product homepage and private invitation-only email/password dashboard share analytics.postdeploy.dev. Administrators manage the installation; Viewers read and export all projects. No public signup or client access boundaries.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Traffic, acquisition, page and named goals, daily conversion rates, bounce, estimated visible duration, comparisons, live traffic, CSV, organisation overview and Insights. Exact populations and unknown legacy duration remain explicit. No stored raw IP, raw user agent or persistent browser identity. Deployment and capacity limits are recorded in PRODUCT_FACTS.md.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the adopted postdeploy. wordmark, icon, Onest/Open Runde typefaces and pale green/ink/lime palette. Plain, specific, calm copy; no invented customers, prices, testimonials or compliance guarantees.

## Evidence on Hand

Working dashboard, explicit sample-data demo, known-population tests, local installation/recovery proofs and bounded read-only history evidence. No public customer proof is supplied.

## Product Principles

- Count useful traffic without persistent browser identification.
- Keep metric definitions exact and visible.
- Group projects without inventing cross-project unique people.
- Keep collection and reports under the operator’s control.
