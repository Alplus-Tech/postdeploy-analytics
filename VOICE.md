# Voice

How PostDeploy Analytics sounds, in the product and anywhere it speaks for itself. Facts come from [PRODUCT_FACTS.md](PRODUCT_FACTS.md); this file decides how they are said.

## Who we are talking to

People who run websites and want clear numbers without a second job: founders, small product and marketing teams, and developers who would rather own their analytics than rent them. They have tried heavy analytics suites and found them slow to read. They care about what brought visitors and what made money, and they are wary of tools that watch people more than they need to.

Analytics products usually speak to this buyer in one of two ways: enterprise depth and dense jargon, or a privacy pitch built on fear. We take neither route. We sound like a capable colleague showing you a clear report.

## The voice

- **Plain.** Short sentences, everyday words. "Visitors", not "users" or "sessions" unless the metric really is sessions.
- **Specific.** Name the number, the page, the source. "Newsletter brought 23% of revenue" beats "great insights into your channels".
- **Calm and confident.** State what the product does and stop. No exclamation marks, no hype.
- **Respectful of visitors.** We count visits; we don't watch people. Say what is counted and leave out the drama.
- **Honest about stage.** Planned features are said as intent. A demo says it is a demo.

The line we come back to: **All the analytics you actually need.** Supporting lines, once the facts back them: no complexity, no surveillance, no expensive infrastructure.

## In the interface

- Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings. No capitals for emphasis.
- Buttons say what happens: "Send invite", "Create key", "Check installation".
- Headings name the thing or the result: "Revenue by channel", "Waiting for the first visit to northwind.example".
- Errors say what went wrong and what to do next, in one or two sentences: "The start date must be on or before the end date."
- Empty states say what belongs there and offer the one action that fills it.
- Destructive actions can be undone, or say plainly what they cannot undo.
- Simulated actions say so where it matters: "No email is sent from this demo."

## Words

| Say | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| visitors, visits | users, eyeballs, traffic personas |
| your Cloudflare account | the cloud, our infrastructure |
| website | property, asset, domain (unless it is the domain) |
| designed to, will (for planned features) | does, guarantees (until it ships) |
| cookieless (once shipped) | GDPR-compliant, fully anonymous |
| sample data, demo | real-time insights (in the demo) |
