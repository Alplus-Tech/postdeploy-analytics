Organisations and projects

An installation holds organisations, and each organisation groups projects: your product, a client’s shop, a documentation site. A project keeps its own website settings and tracking ID.

The organisation home shows combined daily pageviews against the previous period, then ranks each project’s contribution. Exact values sit in tooltips and in an accessible table under the chart.

  • Administrators create, rename and move projects with their full history.
  • Date and exact dimension filters apply to the combined report.
  • Existing tracking IDs and report links keep working when projects move.

Project reports

Overview, Audience and Conversions are computed from stored events for the range you pick: today, 7, 30 or 90 days, 12 months, or custom dates. Compare with the previous period or the same period last year.

Break traffic down by source, referrer, campaign, country, device, browser, page and entry page, and combine filters. The website, range, comparison and filters stay in the address, so any view can be bookmarked or shared with a colleague who has access.

Visit
Ends after 30 minutes without activity, at local midnight, or when the time zone changes.
Bounce
A visit with exactly one pageview and no goal.
Visible duration
An estimate of time the page was actually visible, averaged over measured visits only. Very short glimpses can be missed, so it can read low. Older, unmeasured visits are shown as unknown.
Days and hours
Follow each website’s own time zone.

Goals and conversion rates

A goal is either a page path, such as /thanks, or a named event you send after a successful action. Page goals need no extra code. Named events take one line, and can carry revenue.

// after the order is confirmed postdeploy.track('Purchase', { revenue: 84 });

Completion counts include repeats. Conversion rates divide visitor-days that completed a goal by visitor-days in the same window, so a visitor who buys twice in a day still counts once in the rate.

  • Up to 40 goals per website, including archived ones.
  • Rename or archive a goal; archived history stays reportable.
  • A funnel per website and the most common paths to a purchase.

Insights

When you want to look for patterns, Insights opens from any organisation or project report. It covers traffic trends with comparisons, source and project composition, rankings of pages, landing pages and campaigns, a weekday and hour heatmap, conversion trends and a histogram of measured visit duration.

Ranges reach two years. Long sections load progressively, each with its own progress and retry, and a pending number is shown as pending, never as zero.

  • Top categories plus a complete “Other”, so shares always add up.
  • Every chart keeps an exact table and a CSV download.

Live view and CSV

Live shows visitors in the last five minutes, pageviews per minute and the latest events. Open any visit to see its timeline.

Administrators and Viewers can download project series, goals and tables, and organisation series, projects, goals and totals, as CSV. Each file records its date range, filters, coverage and limits. Unknown durations stay blank, never zero. There is no raw event or visitor export.

Collection controls

Each website has a canonical hostname and up to 20 aliases. Unlisted staging sites and subdomains can’t send data to production, and two projects can’t claim the same hostname.

Exclude up to 20 path prefixes, such as /account, and matching pages are dropped before anything is stored. Choose how long events are kept: 6, 12 or 24 months, or forever. Cleanup runs every hour.

  • Known bots are excluded on new projects.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured by default.
  • A check confirms that a website’s tracking code is sending events.

Team access

Sign-in is by email and password, with no public registration. The first administrator is created with a one-time setup secret that only the person installing it holds.

Administrator
Manages users, organisations, projects, goals and settings, and reads everything.
Viewer
Reads every organisation and project, copies tracking snippets and downloads CSV.
  • With Cloudflare Email configured: invitations and password resets using expiring single-use links.
  • Hashed session tokens, secure cookies for signed-in team members, and CSRF checks.
  • Operator recovery from the command line if every administrator is locked out.

Built for your internal team

Organisations group reports. Every active team member can read every project in the installation.

Use a separate installation when another team needs a separate data boundary. For sharing a report outside the team, export the filtered data as CSV.
