A few useful answers.

Can I use this dashboard?

This installation is private to our team. Existing members can sign in; an administrator invites additional users. To use PostDeploy Analytics for your own team, download and host your own installation.

What does “visitor-days” mean?

Visitors are counted per project and local day. Totals across dates or projects sum those daily counts. They don’t represent distinct people across websites.

Do I need a cookie banner?

The tracker doesn’t use cookies or persistent browser storage. Your legal obligations depend on your website, configuration and jurisdiction; cookieless analytics isn’t a compliance guarantee.

What’s included in the demo?

Interactive reports with clearly labelled sample data. You can explore charts, date ranges and filters without accessing our private analytics.
