Every website you run, in one clear report.

PostDeploy Analytics counts visits, sources and goals across your own and your clients’ websites. It runs as a Worker and a D1 database in your Cloudflare account, with no visitor cookies.

This is everything a pageview sends.

The tracker trims the address before anything leaves the browser. The server then works out country, device and browser, and drops the IP address and user agent before storing the visit.

How counting works

Counted per day

Visitors are counted with a hash whose salt changes every day and is deleted after two. Reports call the result visitor-days, and say so.

Respectful by default

New projects skip known bots and honour Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Exclude private paths before anything is stored.

Stated, not promised

Cookieless analytics is not a compliance guarantee. Your obligations depend on your website, configuration and jurisdiction.

A few useful answers.

Can I sign up for this dashboard?

No. analytics.postdeploy.dev is our team’s private installation, and there is no public signup. To use PostDeploy Analytics, deploy your own copy from the GitHub repository. Your administrators then invite your team.

What does it cost?

The software is free under the MIT license. Running it uses your Cloudflare account, so Cloudflare’s plans, usage pricing and limits apply. Traffic, retention and the number of projects decide how much you use.

What does “visitor-days” mean?

Visitors are counted per project and per local day. Totals across several days or projects add those daily counts together, so they never claim to know how many distinct people visited across websites.

Do I still need a cookie banner?

The tracker uses no cookies or persistent browser storage. Whether you need a banner depends on your website, your configuration and your jurisdiction. Cookieless analytics isn’t a compliance guarantee.

Can my clients log in?

Not today. Everyone with an account can read every project. Administrators manage users, projects and settings; Viewers read and export. Share numbers with clients through CSV exports.

What isn’t built yet?

An API with access keys, an MCP server for AI assistants, client-specific access, public dashboards and payment provider webhooks are planned. Today, revenue comes from postdeploy.track() calls on your website.

What is in the demo?

The full dashboard running on generated sample data for three fictional websites. Nothing is collected, and you can try every report, filter and date range.
